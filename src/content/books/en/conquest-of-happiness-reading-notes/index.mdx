## Overview

Russell analyzes why we are unhappy: abandon self-centeredness, broaden your interests, and engage with life to find real happiness.

## Summary

The key is not escaping life but stepping out of the narrow self: broadening interests, reducing mental drain, staying friendly toward people and things, and finding joy in a calm, moderate life.
