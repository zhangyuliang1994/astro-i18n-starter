> Jobs has been my idol since secondary school. His Stanford speech often encourages me, especially two lines. The first is “stay hungry, stay foolish”; the second is “keep looking, don't settle.” I have always practiced the first—after all, lifelong learning lasts a lifetime. The second feels difficult. I once asked my calligraphy teacher to write a passage for me to hang at home as encouragement: Life is for experiencing, not performing perfection. I am slowly accepting my slowness and ordinariness, allowing myself mistakes and the occasional power outage, blooming fiercely despite regrets. This is the only way to make peace with myself.

## I. Overview
This is the second book in this year's Zhou Zuoluo book club. It was my fourth reading: three times electronically and once on paper. Classics are like that: every reading brings something new. When our company's book club began and everyone was asked to recommend a book, this was my choice.

Jobs's greatness needs no elaboration, and the book itself is excellent. Every time I read it, I get excited. I am also an Apple fan: I own two iPhones, a MacBook Pro, AirPods Pro, an Apple Watch, an Apple TV, and a pair of HomePod mini speakers.

I especially like the Apple ecosystem and its seamless connections. My Apple Watch unlocks my Mac without a password or fingerprint; my iPhone serves as an Apple TV remote, making YouTube searches easier; and the shared clipboard between iPhone and Mac makes transferring information more convenient. There are so many examples that I could talk for two hours without finishing.

## II. Selected Passages
### Design and Simplicity
- Design should seek simplicity while expressing a spirit.
- Design is not only about how a product looks; it must reflect its essence.
- Simplified design should be combined with ease of use.
This may be a major reason Apple fans like its products. Nobody likes complexity; simpler things are easier to control.

- Pursuing simplicity does not mean ignoring complexity, but making the complex simple.
- Why do we think simple is good? With a physical product, we like feeling in control.
We want products that are simple but not simplistic, not products that are good for nothing.

- Asceticism and minimalism sharpen perception. One principle of Buddhist practice is to avoid attachment to material things. Possessions fill life without fulfilling it.
- He believed that less is more and discipline brings joy.
Spiritual abundance matters more. Pursuing possessions is a bottomless pit and can leave us even emptier.

- Deciding what not to do is as important as deciding what to do.
Attention and energy are limited. We should focus on a few important things rather than wanting everything.

- A closed system may be the only way to deliver Apple's celebrated technological Zen. Closed systems have a bad reputation, but they work well and benefit users.
- One of Apple's great advantages is integrating resources, from design, hardware, and software to content.
- True creativity and simplicity come from an integrated product—hardware, software, content, covers, and salespeople—not from opening everything up and letting the parts go their separate ways, as with Windows PCs in the past and Android devices today.
As a software engineer, I like open source. As a user, I like closed systems and dislike tinkering. Leave specialized work to specialists; I only need to do my own work.

### Pursuing Perfection
- Finishing the backs of cabinets and fences matters, even though people cannot see them. He pursued perfection and cared even about unseen places.
- Perfection means giving craftsmanship your full attention even where nobody can see it.
This is somewhat at odds with the previous section, but details sometimes determine success or failure. A good user experience can bring more orders.

- His obsession was a passion for products—for perfect products.
- Passionate craftsmanship means making even the hidden parts beautiful.
- The unseen parts of a product should be as finely made as those on display.
- If you find something wrong, you cannot just ignore it and say, “We'll deal with it later.”
Software development is the same. We cannot endlessly pile garbage code onto a codebase that is already a mess; we need frequent refactoring to address unreasonable aspects of the project.

### Technology and Art
- Jobs always consciously placed himself at the intersection of art and technology.
- Good artists copy ideas; great artists steal inspiration.
This is often quoted. Geniuses are rare, and for ordinary people like us, knowing how to learn from someone else's work is a skill too.

- There should be no distinction between art and applied industrial design.
- Real artists ship.
- The core of the Apple brand: poetry closely connected to engineering; art, creativity, and technology perfectly combined; design that is both striking and simple.
- The purpose of art is to drive ugliness away.

### The Reality Distortion Field
- Turning charisma into persuasion, using the force of personality to entice, coerce, and distort facts.
- Willpower can change reality.
- When he was present, reality was malleable. He could make almost anyone believe almost anything. Once he left, the field gradually faded.
- The reality distortion field combined charismatic rhetoric, an indomitable will, and an eagerness to make reality submit to his intentions.
This is really a flattering description. In today's language, it is psychological manipulation—though that does not work on a low-level workhorse like me.

- He had an extraordinary ability to get what he wanted, judge a person well, and know what to say to win that person's heart.
- Do you want to sell sugar water for the rest of your life, or take the chance to change the world?
A memorable line. Jobs used it to persuade Sculley to become Apple's CEO. But in Jobs's binary view of geniuses and idiots, Sculley was clearly the latter; so, in the end, he drove Sculley out of Apple.

- The reality distortion field can spur people on, but eventually reality always breaks it.
Manipulation does not always succeed. Real-world factors must still be considered.

- We devised a set of signals—touching the nose or pulling an ear. If someone fell into Steve's reality distortion field, we used them to bring that person back to reality.
An anti-manipulation technique that still works today to avoid being played by superiors or bosses.

### Life Experience
- Nobody wants to be lectured, but everyone likes hearing stories.
- Remembering that I will die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking I have something to lose.
Treat every day as if it were the last, so you avoid wasting time on unimportant things.

- Stay hungry. Stay foolish.
- The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do.
- The journey is the reward.
I strongly agree. Producing work regularly, for example, is itself a process of reflection that helps me organize and summarize things better.

- Intuitive understanding and awareness matter more than abstract thought and logical analysis.
- If you are willing to travel far to meet a mentor, one will often appear nearby.
Among any three people, one can be my teacher. Learn more from excellent people around you; you do not necessarily need to travel far.

- In the first thirty years of life, you form habits; in the next thirty, habits form you.
I am already past thirty and still exploring how to build habits. I feel ashamed.

- In innovation, novel ideas are only part of the story; execution is equally important.

## III. Conclusion
One way to remember who you are is to remember who your heroes are. Jobs's hero was Bob Dylan; mine is Jobs.

As we move through life, we need an idol or mentor like this to urge us toward our ideals. We occasionally slide backward, but we remain on the road toward our goals. I am thirty-one this year, and life often feels tedious: repetitive work every weekday, then on days off, idling first and working hard afterward. It is always like this.

But that is life. Romain Rolland said there is only one kind of heroism: loving life after recognizing its truth. May we all keep striving and achieve what we wish for!
