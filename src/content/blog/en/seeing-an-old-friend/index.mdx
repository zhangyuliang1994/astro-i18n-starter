This morning I went to see Xu Bing. A few weeks ago, while scrolling short videos, I came across someone playing table tennis, and thought I should get together with an old friend from college.

We looked for a table tennis hall for an hour, but we could not find one, and in the end we never got to play. When it was time to eat, we went straight to a meal: burgers and fries, and drinks. After the meal we talked about everything, and the conversation went on for hours. When we were done I just felt light, and my chest was no longer tight. We should meet more often from now on.

In the end, my main problem is still work. More precisely, it is that sense of powerlessness in the workplace, and it mainly comes from my side. He actually seemed a little guilty. He is now a technical backbone at some company, busy with the commute every day, works out for an hour after work, and plays games when he has time. I thought of the days in college when we played CF and Overwatch together. He could reach the top 20% to 30% then, and I could not even get into the top 50% to 60%.

I feel like Xianglin Sao, the woman who could only repeat her grievances and never take action.

In the afternoon he also messaged me, saying I did not have to be so pessimistic. He also had a three-month gap himself, and still got 5 offers when he was looking for work.

I think he is right. I should stop complaining and start acting.
